## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

God's Eye View is a browser-based geospatial intelligence console. This extension lets the operator browse and inspect the metro Atlanta place registry, the workflow confirmed by the user.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The registry grows and refreshes from public data. Preserve source attribution, review dates, coordinate limitations, and confirmation requirements when displaying records. Browse the server's paginated catalog; do not download the entire registry into the browser. The user requested deployment of the completed view.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing application, `src/ui/styles/foundation.css`, and the place registry API define the incumbent interface and data contracts. The registry includes curated airport facilities, official GIS records, and recurring Overture releases. Broader audience and product strategy are not defined by this task.
